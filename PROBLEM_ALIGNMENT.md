# Problem Alignment

Requirements traceability for Challenge 6, Urban Air Quality Assistant: each
clause of the brief, the feature that answers it, the code and the tests.

> Create a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate
> severe winter smog and urban air pollution. The application should suggest
> low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable
> alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly
> explaining the direct impact of their choices.

## The same phrases, in the interface

The page uses the brief's wording for its section names, and
[structure-page.dom.test.ts](test/a11y/structure-page.dom.test.ts) asserts that
each phrase is present: "GenAI-powered assistant", "Families and communities",
"Severe winter smog and urban air pollution", "Low-emission daily routines",
"Indoor air quality hacks", "Sustainable alternatives", "Tailored to your
location", "Tailored to your lifestyle" and "The direct impact of your choices".

## Where the numbers come from

Every factor is in [features/impact/factors/](src/features/impact/factors/),
labelled either `published` or `assumption`.

| Published figure                                                              | Source                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Air breathed asleep, at rest, in light activity and in moderate exercise      | US EPA Exposure Factors Handbook (2011), Table 6-2                                 |
| Greenhouse gas per km by petrol car, motorbike, local bus and light rail      | UK DESNZ/DEFRA Greenhouse Gas Conversion Factors 2024                              |
| PM2.5 and CO2 per kg from open garbage burning, PM2.5 from open cooking fires | Akagi et al. (2011), Atmos. Chem. Phys. 11, 4039–4072, Table 2                     |
| N95 filtration                                                                | US CDC/NIOSH                                                                       |
| PM2.5 levels at which the US AQI turns unhealthy                              | US EPA, Final Updates to the Air Quality Index (AQI) for Particulate Matter (2024) |
| Clean air delivered by a box-fan filter                                       | Dal Porto et al. (2022), Aerosol Science and Technology 56(6), 564–572             |

Everything else (infiltration, room sizes, hours, speeds, mask fit, group
uptake, fuel and waste burned, the threshold for protective steps) is an
assumption, shown as one in the interface.

## What is deliberately not done

- The model never calculates. It receives computed facts and returns wording,
  and it is told not to repeat a step's impact: the engine's own impact line is
  printed beside the model's sentence on every card.
- No medical advice: the prompt forbids diagnosis and medication, and the page
  says the guidance is general.
- No accounts and no storage of profiles.
